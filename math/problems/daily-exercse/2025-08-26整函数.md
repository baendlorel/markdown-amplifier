# 2025-08-26 整函数

$f(z)$是整函数，$f(z)=f(z-z^2)$，求证$f(z)$必为常数。

_proof_

利用柯西-黎曼方程，记$u$和$v$是实值函数，我们有

$$ f(z)=u(x,y)+v(x,y)i $$

$$ \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}=0 $$

$$ \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}=0 $$

由于$f(z-z^2)$也是解析的，因此先进行$z-z^2$有

$$ z=x+yi \implies z-z^2=(x-x^2+y^2)+(y-2xy)i $$

再套入柯西-黎曼方程，记$s=x-x^2+y^2$和$t=y-2xy$有

$$ \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial s}-\frac{\partial v(s,t)}{\partial t}=0 $$

$$ \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial v(s,t)}{\partial s}=0 $$

但是，同样因为$f(z-z^2)$是解析的，因此它还必须满足
$$ \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial v(s,t)}{\partial y}=0 $$

$$ \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial v(s,t)}{\partial x}=0 $$

$$ \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial x}= \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial s} \cdot \frac{\partial s}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u(s,t)}{\partial t} \cdot \frac{\partial t}{\partial x} $$
